## About the role

Avon Chambers is looking for a corporate associate with three to five years of transactional experience to join a practice where every deal is partner-led. You will work directly with Helena Marchetti and the corporate team on live transactions from term sheet through closing — not in a rotating staffing pool, and not handed diligence work with no visibility into the deal's negotiation.

## What you'll work on

- Mergers and acquisitions, buy-side and sell-side, across fintech, healthcare services and industrial manufacturing clients
- Venture and debt financings, from priced rounds to recapitalisations
- Disclosure schedule preparation and diligence coordination
- Direct client contact earlier than is typical for this level of seniority

## What we're looking for

- 3–5 years at a law firm or in-house, with substantive M&A or financing transaction experience
- Admitted to practice in New York (or eligible for admission by waiver)
- Comfortable drafting primary transaction documents, not just reviewing them
- Judgment about when to escalate and when to resolve something independently — we do not micromanage, and we do not want to

## Compensation and structure

Compensation is benchmarked against comparable boutique New York corporate practices and discussed openly during the interview process rather than after an offer is made. This is a full-time role based in our lower Manhattan office with a hybrid working arrangement.

## How to apply

Send a resume and a short note on the most complex transaction you've worked on — what made it complex, and what you were responsible for — to [careers@avonchambers.com](mailto:careers@avonchambers.com). No cover letter template required; we read what you actually send.
